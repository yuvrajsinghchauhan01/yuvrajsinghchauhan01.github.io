TubeScope

A personal YouTube research tool

TubeScope is a private, self-hosted tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service and has no user accounts.

What it does

  • Keyword ideas and keyword scoring from public YouTube data.
  • Finding videos that perform far above their channel's usual views.
  • Tracking public statistics of chosen channels over time.
  • Showing the owner's own channel analytics (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources) after the owner signs in with Google.

Google sign-in

TubeScope asks only for read-only access to YouTube and YouTube Analytics (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly). It cannot upload, edit or delete anything. All data stays on the owner's computer.

Privacy policy

TubeScope uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
